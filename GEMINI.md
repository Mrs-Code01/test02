# No Em Dash Policy

**Trigger:** Before creating, writing, or editing any page, document, article, or content output.

**Rule:** Never use an em dash (—) anywhere in the output. This is a hard constraint, not a stylistic suggestion.

**Enforcement steps:**
- Before finalizing any content, scan the full text for the em dash character (—).
- If found, replace it using one of these alternatives, chosen for what reads most naturally in context:
  - A period, splitting the sentence in two
  - A comma
  - Parentheses
  - A colon (for a list or explanation)
  - The words "and," "but," or "because" as a connector
- Do not substitute a double hyphen (--) or en dash (–) as a workaround. Rewrite the sentence structure instead.
- Re-read the final draft once, specifically checking for em dashes, before delivering it.

**Applies to:** All page types — blog posts, landing pages, proposals, portfolio entries, gig descriptions, LinkedIn content, and any other written deliverable.
